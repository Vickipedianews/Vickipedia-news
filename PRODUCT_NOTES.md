# Vickipedia News — Product direction

## Experience principle

**Editorial substance first.** Vickipedia's identity should communicate authority, curiosity, and independence, rather than borrowing the visual language of a SaaS dashboard or generic WordPress theme. The design pairs confident newspaper-style typography with restrained violet, crisp rules, considered whitespace and intentionally tiered story modules.

## Reader journeys

1. **Discover:** curated cover + newest reports + sections with visible editorial hierarchy.
2. **Explore:** persistent category navigation, keyboard search and archives.
3. **Read:** strong headlines, a readable measure, accessible contrast, progress, table of contents, sources as links, and related stories.
4. **Return:** save stories locally, use dark mode, subscribe to the brief.

## Editorial journeys

1. Open Studio and assess the queue.
2. Create/edit story, attach byline/section/cover, write and format.
3. Preview and check reading time.
4. Keep draft private or publish; choose lead/breaking visibility.
5. Manage media, contributors and institutional pages.

## Before public launch

- Replace demo coverage and placeholder editorial claims.
- Commission and license a coherent photojournalism system (do not reuse decorative graphics as real event photographs).
- Obtain genuine logo/brand assets, author portraits, real contact channels, privacy policy and editorial standards.
- Define publisher workflow: reporting, fact-checking, copy editing, legal review, corrections, approval and archiving.
- Add metrics, observability and backups, then test with real traffic and real journalists.

## Product v2 candidates

- Multi-user RBAC and assignments; editorial calendar, assignments and approvals.
- Version history with diffs, rollbacks, corrections, source annotations and scheduling.
- Real rich-text block editor with quotations, inline media, tables, galleries and embeds.
- Image processing, responsive formats, automatic credits, asset rights and CDN.
- Email verification, segmentation, campaigns and consent management.
- Analytics, personalized recommendations, alerts, topic-following and synced reading lists.
- Accessibility audit, SEO schema markup, structured newsroom metadata, content provenance and performance budget.
